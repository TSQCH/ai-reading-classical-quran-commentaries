Counting rule of the paper (v12), to apply strictly and identically to every position.

Each mufassir's position on a phrase is one of five types:
- prefers: he gives more than one view, then explicitly chooses one (a word of choice such as al-sawab, awla, al-azhar, al-aqrab, al-mukhtar, al-sahih, asahh, arjah, aqwa when it ranks one view over the others), OR he argues explicitly for one view against the others so that his choice is clear (e.g. calling every other view weak/far-fetched while keeping one).
- single: he states only ONE meaning in his own voice and gives no sign that other views exist. If that one meaning is introduced with "qila" (it is said), he is reporting, not holding: it is NOT single (record lists_no_choice).
- combines: he states that the phrase covers all/both views, or that they do not conflict. EXCEPTION: if the general/comprehensive meaning is itself one of the views listed for the phrase (a view label meaning "general", "all", "every ..."), then choosing it is a vote for that view: record prefers (if with a word of choice/argument) or single, holding that view's id.
- lists_no_choice: he gives several views and does not choose.
- silent: does not address it.

Not a choice (stay lists_no_choice): calling a view "hasan" or "qawi" without placing it above the others; his own gloss stated before he reports the dispute, without a word of choice; attributing a view to the majority (al-jumhur, al-akthar, akthar al-mufassirin) without adopting it in his own words; quoting someone else's preference (e.g. "wa-ikhtarahu al-Tabari", "qala al-Wahidi: wa-l-awla") without endorsing it; a preference word that concerns a reading (qira'a), grammar (i'rab) or another question, not the meaning in dispute.
Also check that the view ids in "holds" match what the text says (the right view label), especially for single/prefers.
Only prefers and single are votes.
